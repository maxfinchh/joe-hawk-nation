Community Guidelines

Last updated: March 31, 2026

We want Joe Hawk Nation to be a place for sports discussion without abuse.

Do not post:

  • Harassment or threats
  • Hate speech or discriminatory abuse
  • Sexually explicit content
  • Graphic violence
  • Spam or scams
  • Illegal content
  • Impersonation or deceptive behavior

Reporting and Blocking

Users can report comments and block other users in the app. Reported content may be reviewed and removed.

Enforcement

We may remove content or suspend accounts that violate these guidelines.

Moderation

We aim to review reported objectionable content within 24 hours.
