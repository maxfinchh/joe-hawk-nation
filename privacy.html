Privacy Policy for Joe Hawk Nation

Last updated: March 31, 2026

Joe Hawk Nation values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Email address and account login information
  • User ID and account status
  • Comments, likes, reports, blocked-user preferences, and other content you submit in the app
  • Purchase and premium-access status
  • Basic technical and usage information needed to operate the app

How We Use Information

  • To create and manage user accounts
  • To provide access to app features, including premium content
  • To process and restore purchases
  • To moderate content, review reports, and help keep the app safe
  • To improve app functionality and user experience
  • To respond to support or account-related requests

Third-Party Services

We use third-party services to operate the app, including Firebase for authentication and database services, RevenueCat for purchase management, and Apple for in-app purchases.

Purchases

Premium purchases are processed by Apple through in-app purchases. We do not collect or store your full payment card information.

User Content and Moderation

If you post comments or other content in the app, that information may be visible to other users. We may review reported content and remove content or accounts that violate our rules.

Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your account from within the app. When your account is deleted, we will delete or anonymize associated data as required for app operation, security, legal compliance, or legitimate business purposes.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the app, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our policies.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or request information about your personal data.

Children’s Privacy

Joe Hawk Nation is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
